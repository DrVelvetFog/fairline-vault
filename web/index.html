The honest pivot

We started losing — so we flipped sides.

FairLine began as an ML bot betting on direction. Live on testnet, it lost money: the spread was wider than the model's edge. On-chain, the lost money went to one place — the liquidity pool. The house. So we became the house, and turned the model into a defensive risk gate.

Before · directional bot
41% win

−783 dUSDC live. A 63%-accurate model still loses when the 2% spread beats the edge. The directional sleeve survives — small, capped, reported honestly.

After · be the house
+0.32%

LP-primary: supply liquidity, earn the spread the losers pay. The ML/volatility signal now gates exposure against risk instead of gambling with it.

How it works

Three parts, one transparent system.

💧

LP-primary engine

Supplies ~70% of capital to DeepBook Predict's pool to earn the structural house edge. Sticky by design — it sizes position by risk rather than churning in and out.

🧠

ML risk gate

A trained directional model (5-fold CV) used defensively: when a large move is likely, FairLine scales liquidity down. The losing alpha model becomes a useful risk model.

🏦

Multi-user vault

Our own Move contract: deposit dUSDC, receive FLP shares priced at NAV, withdraw pro-rata. Real depositors, real TVL — anyone can join the house.
