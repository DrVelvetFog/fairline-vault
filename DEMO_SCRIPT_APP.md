# FairLine — App Walkthrough Script (mobile user experience)

For the **second** video: a real user using the app on a phone, with an Evan
voiceover explaining how to use it. ~2:00–2:30. Record portrait (vertical) on your
phone, screen-recording the browser at `fairline-vault.netlify.app`.

Pace to these beats; loose timing is fine (narration syncs to scenes, not the clock).

| # | On screen (do this) | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The landing page, scroll briefly | "This is FairLine — a liquidity vault for DeepBook's prediction markets on Sui. Let's use it." |
| 2 | Tap **Launch app** | "One tap opens the app — right in the phone's browser, no install." |
| 3 | The app loads; rest on the **Vault & Market** card | "Before you deposit, you can see everything live: the vault's total value, the house edge it's earned, and current market conditions — Bitcoin's price, volatility, and the regime." |

| 6 | The **Your Position** card appears | "Now you see your position: your shares, their value, and your accrued profit or loss — computed from your own on-chain deposits." |

| 8 | Show the ✓ confirmation + tap the tx link | "…and you've minted FLP shares, priced at the vault's net asset value. There's the transaction, on-chain." |
